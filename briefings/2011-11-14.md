# Weekly Briefing — Week of 2011-11-14

*Period: `2011-11-14` to `2011-11-20` (GBP) · Summary source: `llm_verified`*

Across product categories and geographic regions, the top drivers for the week were Bags and Netherlands, respectively. Overall, customer engagement remained robust with 569 customers and a strong repeat customer rate of 93.0%, marking positive momentum across multiple key performance indicators compared to historical averages.
