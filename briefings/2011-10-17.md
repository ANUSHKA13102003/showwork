# Weekly Briefing — Week of 2011-10-17

*Period: `2011-10-17` to `2011-10-23` (GBP) · Summary source: `llm_verified`*

During the week of 2011-10-17, retail performance was notably strong, driven by significant gains in sales value despite steady transaction volumes. Net revenue reached £259,054, marking a +26.8% increase compared to the previous week and outperforming the prior 4-week average by +0.9% against a baseline of £256,666. Order volume stood at 446 transactions, shifting slightly by -0.4% week-over-week, while the average order value climbed substantially to £592.73, representing a +20.0% week-over-week growth and a +2.7% rise compared to the 4-week average of £577.33. The primary growth drivers for the period were the Frames and Clocks product category and strong international demand originating from the Netherlands. Additional support came from returning customer types, ensuring a robust overall performance across key commercial metrics.
