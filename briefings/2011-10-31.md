# Weekly Briefing — Week of 2011-10-31

*Period: `2011-10-31` to `2011-11-06` (GBP) · Summary source: `llm_verified`*

During the week of 2011-10-31, net revenue reached £288,344, marking a strong week-over-week increase of +23.1% compared to £234,203 the previous week. This performance also exceeded the prior 4-week average of £249,454 by +15.6%. Growth was supported by 527 orders, representing a +1.5% change from 519 orders last week and a +10.9% rise against the 4-week average of 475. Average order value climbed to £555.50, up +18.8% week-over-week and +1.4% versus the 4-week average of £547.85. The primary category driver was Kitchen and Dining, while the United Kingdom led as the top country driver, and returning customers were the leading customer type driver.
