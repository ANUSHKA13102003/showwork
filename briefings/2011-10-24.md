# Weekly Briefing — Week of 2011-10-24

*Period: `2011-10-24` to `2011-10-30` (GBP) · Summary source: `llm_verified`*

For the retail week ending 2011-10-24, net revenue reached £234,203, representing a week-over-week decline of -9.6% compared to £259,054 the previous week, and performing at -3.0% below the prior four-week average of £241,493. Customer engagement remained strong with 419 customers, a +15.1% increase, and a repeat customer rate of 88.8%. Performance drivers included Lighting as the top category driver, while the Netherlands acted as the primary country driver despite a previous decline.
